Touchscreen intake

Lets whoever is on comms send an incoming-patient report from the unit's touchscreen straight to the triage board. The touchscreen can only send reports; it can't open any patient record.

Without a PIN, anyone standing at the screen can send a report. Reports are labelled as touchscreen reports in the audit trail, with the name or call sign the sender types.
