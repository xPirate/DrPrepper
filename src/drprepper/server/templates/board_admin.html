Status board

Open the board

A read-only wall screen: room status and patient counts, refreshed every 10 seconds. Plug a TV into the Pi's second HDMI port, or open it on any screen on the unit's Wi-Fi with a display key.

What the board shows

Off by default: with names off the board shows rooms and counts only, nothing that identifies anyone. Turn names on only when every board screen is inside the facility, out of public view. Every screen shows "NAMES SHOWN" while it's on.

Display keys

A key lets one screen show the board without anyone signing in. It opens the board and nothing else. Open the link once on the screen; the browser remembers it.
